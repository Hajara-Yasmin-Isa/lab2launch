Lab2Launch

Lab2Launch is a student-led community of graduate researchers at the University of Illinois Urbana-Champaign who want to take their work beyond publications and prototypes. Built by students, for students. We bring together curious minds who are ready to explore entrepreneurship, commercialization, and real-world impact.

Join the mailing list → Get involved

Who is this for?

If you're curious about entrepreneurship, or about making more impact with your research, this is for you. No membership, no application, no pitch deck required. We want to create a low-pressure environment to learn and to meet others who are interested in the same things.

Event formats so far

01 / LUNCHES

Founder lunches

A table, a dozen grad students, and someone who has already done it. The parts of the story that never make it into a talk — the co-founder split, the first customer, the year it nearly died.

02 / FIRESIDES

Fireside chats

One guest, one interviewer, no slides. Half structured conversation, half questions from the room — closer to an interview than a talk.

03 / SEMINARS

Startup seminars

Longer-form sessions with people running deep-tech companies, open to the whole department. Databases, petabyte analytics, quantum memory — the kind of company that starts as a research problem.
