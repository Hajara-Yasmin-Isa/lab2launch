Welcome to Lab2Launch!

Lab2Launch is a student-led community of graduate researchers at UIUC who want to take their work beyond publications and prototypes. Built by students, for students, we bring together curious minds who are ready to explore entrepreneurship, commercialization, and real-world impact. Our mission is to help you turn breakthrough ideas into reality by connecting you with the mentorship, network, and resources needed to launch deep-tech ventures and shape the next generation of innovation.
